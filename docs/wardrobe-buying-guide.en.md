## 2. What to buy

Two wardrobes, as decided — master plus the medium bedroom.

| Room | Clear wall available | Buy | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bedroom 8 (master)** — 16'0" × 11'1½" | ~6 ft beside the dress/toilet door | **6 ft W × 7 ft H × 2 ft D, 3 doors** | The dress room (8A) already holds overflow; 6 ft leaves 5 ft of walkway |
| **Bedroom 7** — 11'0" × 15'0" | ~11 ft on the long wall | **6 ft W × 7 ft H × 2 ft D, 3 doors** | Long wall opposite the bed; comfortably fits |
| **Bedroom 5** — 13'6" × 10'6" | ~10'6" | *Optional:* 4 ft W × 6'6" H | Only if a tenant later needs a third; matched style, smaller |

**Don't buy bigger than 6 ft** in these rooms. The gain in storage is small, and you lose the walkway and the door swing.

### Internal layout to ask for
- **1 full-width hanging rod** at 5'6"–6' height
- **4–5 shelves** (adjustable)
- **1 locker / 1 drawer** for documents
- **1 loft shelf** at the top if you buy 7 ft
- Split: 60% hanging / 40% shelves is the practical mix for a family

## 3. Specification to insist on

| Part | Buy this | Reject this |
|---|---|---|
| Carcass | **18 mm plywood** or **HDHMR** | Particle board / "engineered wood" |
| Back panel | **6 mm** board | 3 mm hardboard |
| Shutters | 18 mm, laminate / PVC / lacquered glass | 12 mm shutter, paper laminate |
| Hinges | Branded — Hettich / Ebco / Hafele | No-name hinges |
| Hanging rod | 25 mm steel or aluminium, full width | Thin plated rod, half width |
| Edge banding | 1–2 mm PVC on every edge | Unsealed / painted edges |
| Depth | **24" (2 ft)** | 18" — useless for hangers |
| Height | 6'6"–7'0" | Under 6 ft |
| Base | Plinth or legs | Sitting flat on the floor (mop water) |

Soft-close is **not** required — standard branded hinges are fine and cheaper.

## 6. Making it look built-in

Readymade looks cheap mostly because of **gaps**, not the unit itself.

- Pick the width that leaves only **2–3 inches** at the wall, not 12.
- Choose a **neutral laminate** close to the kitchen finish (off-white / beige / light grey).
- Fill the gap above with a **matching loft box** — a local carpenter will do it for ₹4,000–8,000 per unit and it transforms the look.
- Run a thin **silicone/trim bead** along the wall and ceiling line.
- **Anchor the unit to the wall** — tall wardrobes tip over. This is a safety issue with children.

## 7. Measure before ordering

- [ ] Clear wall length, between skirting and door jamb (not the room dimension)
- [ ] Ceiling height, and height under any beam
- [ ] Door swing — the wardrobe must not block it
- [ ] Switchboards and AC points on that wall
- [ ] Window position
- [ ] Lift/staircase width — **prefer flat-pack / knock-down units**, fully assembled ones often will not go up
- [ ] Floor level — get adjustable feet if the floor slopes

## 8. Order of work

1. Contractor finishes kitchen + door laminates.
2. Measure walls (after painting, if painting is planned).
3. Order wardrobes — allow 1–3 weeks for delivery.
4. Install, anchor to wall, add loft box.
5. Photograph for the rental listing.
