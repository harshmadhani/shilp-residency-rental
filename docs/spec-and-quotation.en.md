# Material Specification & Suggested Quotation

**Property:** Flat No. ____, C Block (3-Series), Shilp Residency, Gota, SG Highway, Ahmedabad — 3 BHK, 2,360 sq ft
**Date:** 20 October 2026

This note explains the materials chosen, why they suit a rental used for many years, and gives the suggested quotation derived from the contractor's measurements.

## 1. Types of wood / board available

| Material | Water resistance | Relative cost | Suitable for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Particle board (chipboard) | Poor — swells permanently | Lowest | Never wet areas. Avoid |
| MDF (plain) | Poor | Low | Shutters in dry areas only |
| MR-MDF / HDF / HDHMR | Medium to high | Medium to high | Shutters, dry cabinets |
| Plywood — IS 303 (MR) | Medium | Medium | Dry interiors |
| **Plywood — IS 710 (BWR/BWP)** | **High — boiling-water-proof** | Higher | **Kitchens, bathrooms, wardrobes** |
| Marine ply (Gurjan core) | Highest | Highest | Wet zones |
| Block board | Medium | Medium | Long shutters, doors |
| Solid wood (teak, sheesham, sal) | Varies | High to very high | Loose furniture, doors |

**Grade codes:** `IS 303` = MR (moisture resistant, dry areas). `IS 710` = BWR/BWP (boiling-water resistant/proof — the correct grade for a kitchen).

## 2. Specification chosen for multi-year tenancy

Tenants are hard on cabinets, and this flat may be let for 5–10 years. The build therefore targets durability, not the cheapest number:

| Element | Specification | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen carcass | **18 mm branded ply, IS 710 (BWP)** | Survives steam, sink splashes and humidity |
| Back / wall-side panel | **12 mm** | Stiffer, resists sagging |
| Laminate | **1 mm, both faces, branded** | Thin 0.8 mm inner laminate chips and peels |
| Edge banding | **2 mm PVC, every edge sealed** | The single biggest factor in cabinet life |
| Hardware | **Ebco / Hettich** hinges and telescopic channels | No-name hardware is the number-one failure point |
| Sink cabinet | **Waterproof lining + silicone seal** | Killer of cheap cabinets |
| Brand | Century / Greenply / Gurjan-core | Verifiable, ISI-marked, invoice supplied |

**Not acceptable:** particle board, plain MDF carcass, unsealed edges, no-name hardware.

## 3. Wardrobes — built-in vs readymade

Wardrobes are **out of the contractor's scope** and will be bought readymade. Room-by-room sizes and the full buying spec are in **[wardrobe-buying-guide.en.md](wardrobe-buying-guide.en.md)**.

| Option | Cost for 2 | Fit | Durability | Movability |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Built-in, contractor | ~₹1,42,000 | Exact wall-to-wall | High (IS 710 ply) | Fixed |
| Readymade, branded (Godrej Interio / Durian / @home) | ₹52,000–88,000 | Standard sizes; may leave gaps | Medium | **Movable** |
| Readymade, budget online (particle board) | ₹20,000–40,000 | Standard sizes | Low — swells | Movable |

**Verdict:** readymade is **materially cheaper** (roughly ₹55,000–90,000 saved) and **fits the plan better** — it is movable, so when you rebuild the flat you can move or resell it instead of losing it. The trade-offs are: standard sizes may not fit wall-to-wall, less storage per room, and it looks less "premium" in photos. For a semi-furnished rental that is an acceptable trade; just buy **branded**, not the cheapest particle-board units.

**From the unit plan**, Bedroom 7 is the master (12'0" × 16'0") and takes a **7 ft × 7 ft × 2 ft, 4-door** unit; Bedroom 8 (15'0" × 11'0") takes **6 ft × 7 ft × 2 ft, 3-door**. Bedroom 5 (10'0" × 12'0") is left free as a study / guest room.
