## Should you buy better fans? Yes — and here is the maths

Fans stay in the flat across tenancies. If you keep them 10 years, a ₹900 difference per fan is **₹75 a year**. That is cheap for a better motor, a longer warranty, and a lower bill if you ever move back in.

But "better" should mean four specific things, not a fancier look:

1. **Warranty length.** This is where the cheap pick falls down — the Crompton Ariya Briz carries only **1 year**. Havells gives 2 (door-step on the REO), Atomberg gives 2+1.
2. **BLDC motor.** 33 W against 50 W. If you occupy later, that is roughly **₹400 a year per fan** at ₹8/unit.
3. **No remote.** Remotes disappear with tenants, and a replacement is a phone call to you. Switch-operated is better for a rental.
4. **Service network.** Havells and Crompton have the deepest Ahmedabad coverage. Atomberg is thinner but workable.

That points at one fan: the **Havells REO BLDC 1200 mm at ₹2,850** — 5-star, 33 W, switch-operated, 2-year door-step warranty, Havells service in the city.

**Where I deliberately stayed cheap**

- Battens, bath lights, night lamps, curtain pipes and SS 304 accessories. These are commodity items — a tenant will not notice the difference and none of them fail early.
- Local SS 304 instead of Jaquar: identical at three feet, a third of the price.

**Where I deliberately did not**

- **No 1-year-warranty fans.** Saving ₹500 now to lose the warranty at year two is the wrong trade when you keep the asset.
- **No remotes.** They go missing, and the fan is useless to a tenant who cannot find it.
- **No ₹8,000 decorative fans.** They buy nothing in a rental.

**Rental-proofing (₹1,000, do it):** keep **2 spare battens and 1 spare fan** in the flat. When something fails, it is swapped the same day instead of waiting on a warranty claim you have to chase from another city.

## 15. Spec rules that actually matter

- **Fans: 2-year warranty minimum, BLDC if the price gap is under ₹1,000.**
- **Bathroom lights: IP44 minimum.**
- **Bath accessories: SS 304, never SS 202.**
- **Battens: 4000K neutral white** for a home; 6500K only if you don't care how the photos look.
- **Exhaust: match the cut-out** — 150 mm toilets, 200 mm kitchen.
- **Curtain rods: 25 mm (1"), brackets every 3 ft, 6–8" overhang** each side of the window.

## 16. What not to buy online

- Exhaust fans — fit risk, and the hole must be measured first
- Long curtain pipes — length and bracket quality
- Mirrors — breakage
- Anything you need **today**

## 17. Order of work

1. Measure the exhaust cut-outs and every window width.
2. Visit the Naranpura dealer with these Amazon prices on your phone — fans, battens, exhausts.
3. Order online only the small items: night lamps, IP44 bath lights, Essco accessories.
4. Buy locally: long curtain pipes, mirrors, SS 304 accessories.
5. Electrician does a **point check** — what exists, what needs a new point.
6. Install fans and lights → exhausts → curtain rods → bathroom fittings.
7. Keep invoices and warranty cards in the tenant file.
