Grade codes: IS 303 = MR (moisture resistant, dry areas). IS 710 = BWR/BWP (boiling-water resistant/proof — the correct grade for a kitchen).

2. Specification chosen for multi-year tenancy

Tenants are hard on cabinets, and this flat may be let for 5–10 years. The build therefore targets durability, not the cheapest number:

ElementSpecificationWhy
Kitchen carcass18 mm branded ply, IS 710 (BWP)Survives steam, sink splashes and humidity
Wardrobe carcass18 mm branded ply, IS 710 preferred (IS 303 minimum)Bedrooms are dry, but MR grade still ages better
Back / wall-side panel12 mmStiffer, resists sagging
Laminate1 mm, both faces, brandedThin 0.8 mm inner laminate chips and peels
Edge banding2 mm PVC, every edge sealedThe single biggest factor in cabinet life
HardwareEbco / Hettich hinges and telescopic channelsNo-name hardware is the number-one failure point
Sink cabinetWaterproof lining + silicone sealKiller of cheap cabinets
BrandCentury / Greenply / Gurjan-coreVerifiable, ISI-marked, invoice supplied

Not acceptable: particle board, plain MDF carcass, unsealed edges, no-name hardware.
